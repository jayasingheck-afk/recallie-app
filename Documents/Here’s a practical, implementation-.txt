## 2. Maths sequencing rules

### High-level design principles

- Follow **strand-based progression** within each year/stage:
  - VIC: Number → Algebra (often taught together), then Measurement & Space, then Statistics & Probability. [victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu](https://victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu.au/mathematics/mathematics/introduction/structure)
  - NSW: Number and algebra, Measurement and space, Statistics and probability, with explicit subareas (e.g., additive/multiplicative relations). [v8.australiancurriculum.edu](https://v8.australiancurriculum.edu.au/media/7103/english_scope_and_sequence_f-6.pdf)
- Within each strand, move from:
  - Concrete → representational → abstract  
  - Simple → complex (e.g., within 20 → within 100 → within 1000)  
  - Single-step → multi-step problems  
- Use **weekly “focus skills”** (1–2 per strand) so that over a term you cover the key content descriptors for that year.

Assume a **10-week term** model; you can adjust to local school calendars.

***

### VIC Maths: weekly “new skill” logic

For each year level (Level 1–6), maintain a **sequence of focus skills per strand**. Below is a representative pattern; you’ll map each to specific AC/VIC content codes in your database.

#### Years 1–2 (Level 1–2)

**Number (dominant strand)** – typical weekly focus:

- Week 1–2: Counting sequences, number names, ordering to 20/100; subitising small collections. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)
- Week 3–4: Place value to 20/100 (tens and ones), representing numbers in different ways. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)
- Week 5–6: Addition and subtraction within 20 (facts, strategies, word problems). [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)
- Week 7–8: Addition and subtraction within 100 (two-digit + one-digit, two-digit + two-digit without/with regrouping). [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)
- Week 9–10: Introduction to multiplication as equal groups/repeated addition; simple patterns. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)

**Algebra (light, integrated with Number):**

- Weeks 3–4, 7–8: Simple repeating and increasing/decreasing patterns; continue and create patterns. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)

**Measurement and Geometry (Space):**

- Weeks 2–3: Length (compare, order, informal units). [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)
- Weeks 4–5: Mass (compare, informal units).
- Weeks 6–7: Time (hour/half-hour on analog/digital clocks).
- Weeks 8–9: 2D shapes and 3D objects (features, sorting, spatial language). [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)

**Statistics and Probability:**

- Probability often minimal in Years 1–2; focus on simple chance language (“likely”, “unlikely”).
- Statistics: simple data collection and interpretation (picture graphs, simple tables) in Weeks 5–6 or 9–10.

**Weekly “new skill” rule (VIC Years 1–2):**

Each week, mark as “new”:

- 1 Number focus skill (main thread for the week).
- 0–1 Algebra/pattern skill (if aligned with Number).
- 1 Measurement/Space skill (rotating sub-strand).
- 0–1 Statistics/Probability skill (every 2–3 weeks, not every week).

Example: Year 2, Week 5

- New Number skill: “Add and subtract within 20 using mental strategies” (AC9M2N0x / VIC equivalent).
- New Measurement skill: “Tell time to the hour and half-hour”.
- Optional new Statistics skill: “Interpret simple picture graphs”.

#### Years 3–4 (Level 3–4)

**Number and Algebra:**

- Week 1–2: Place value to 1000; represent and order 3-digit numbers. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)
- Week 3–4: Addition and subtraction within 1000 (with/without regrouping), including word problems. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)
- Week 5–6: Multiplication concepts (equal groups, arrays), facts for 2, 3, 5, 10; link to division as sharing/grouping. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)
- Week 7–8: Multiplication facts up to 10×10; simple division facts; multi-step problems. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)
- Week 9–10: Fractions (unit fractions, equivalence basics), decimals introduction (Year 4). [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)

**Measurement and Space:**

- Weeks 2–3: Length (formal units: m, cm), perimeter basics.
- Weeks 4–5: Area (informal → formal units, rectangles).
- Weeks 6–7: Mass and capacity (g, kg, mL, L).
- Weeks 8–9: Time (minutes, timetables), 2D/3D shape properties, symmetry, transformations. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)

**Statistics and Probability:**

- Probability: simple experiments, language of chance, list outcomes. [victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu](https://victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu.au/mathematics/mathematics/introduction/structure)
- Statistics: column graphs, interpreting data, simple surveys. [victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu](https://victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu.au/mathematics/mathematics/introduction/structure)

**Weekly “new skill” rule (VIC Years 3–4):**

Each week, mark as “new”:

- 1 Number/Algebra focus skill (main thread).
- 1 Measurement/Space skill.
- 1 Statistics/Probability skill every 2–3 weeks.
- Additional Algebra (patterns, simple rules) integrated into Number weeks.

Example: Year 4, Week 6

- New Number skill: “Multiply two-digit by one-digit numbers using strategies”.
- New Measurement skill: “Measure and compare capacity using mL and L”.
- New Statistics skill: “Construct and interpret simple column graphs”.

#### Years 5–6 (Level 5–6)

**Number and Algebra:**

- Week 1–2: Large whole numbers (up to 10,000/100,000), place value, rounding. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)
- Week 3–4: Addition/subtraction with larger numbers; mental and written strategies. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)
- Week 5–6: Multiplication (multi-digit), division with/without remainders. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)
- Week 7–8: Fractions (equivalence, operations with like/unlike denominators), decimals (place value, operations). [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)
- Week 9–10: Percentages (basic), links to fractions/decimals; simple algebraic expressions (Year 6). [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines)

**Measurement and Space:**

- Weeks 2–3: Perimeter and area of rectangles/triangles; units conversion.
- Weeks 4–5: Volume and capacity (prisms, informal → formal).
- Weeks 6–7: Angles, 2D shape properties, transformations, coordinates.
- Weeks 8–9: Time (24-hour time, time zones), more complex measurement problems.

**Statistics and Probability:**

- Probability: simple fractions for likelihood, experimental vs theoretical. [victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu](https://victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu.au/mathematics/mathematics/introduction/structure)
- Statistics: data collection, displays (column, line, pie), mean/mode basics. [victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu](https://victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu.au/mathematics/mathematics/introduction/structure)

**Weekly “new skill” rule (VIC Years 5–6):**

Each week:

- 1 Number/Algebra focus skill (main thread).
- 1 Measurement/Space skill.
- 1 Statistics/Probability skill every 2–3 weeks.
- Integrate Algebra (expressions, simple equations) into Number weeks, especially Year 6.

## 3. English sequencing rules (VIC & NSW)

Both states follow the **Australian Curriculum** structure for English, with three strands:

- **Language** (grammar, vocabulary, spelling, punctuation)  
- **Literature** (texts, themes, characters, literary devices)  
- **Literacy** (reading, viewing, writing, speaking, listening) [victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu](https://victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu.au/mathematics/mathematics-version-2-0/introduction/structure)

Content is sequenced by year level (F–6) with increasing complexity in:

- Text types: narrative, informative, persuasive, poetic, multimodal. [victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu](https://victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu.au/mathematics/mathematics-version-2-0/introduction/structure)
- Reading: decoding → fluency → comprehension (literal → inferential → evaluative). [victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu](https://victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu.au/mathematics/mathematics-version-2-0/introduction/structure)
- Writing: sentence → paragraph → multi-paragraph texts; planning, drafting, editing. [victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu](https://victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu.au/mathematics/mathematics-version-2-0/introduction/structure)
- Language: phonics/phonemic awareness (early years) → morphology, syntax, metalanguage. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0020/262631/Exposure-Draft-Childrens-Online-Privacy-Code.pdf)

You can use a **common sequence for VIC and NSW**, tagging each skill with AC codes and optionally state-specific phrasing.

### Years 1–2

**Reading (Literacy):**

- Weeks 1–3: Phonics revision, high-frequency words, decoding simple texts.  
- Weeks 4–6: Fluency practice, literal comprehension (who/what/where/when).  
- Weeks 7–10: Simple inferential questions, retelling stories, identifying main idea. [victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu](https://victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu.au/mathematics/mathematics-version-2-0/introduction/structure)

**Writing (Literacy):**

- Weeks 1–3: Sentence structure, capital letters, full stops, simple narratives.  
- Weeks 4–6: Writing short informative texts (labels, captions, simple paragraphs).  
- Weeks 7–10: Simple persuasive texts (opinion + reason), basic editing. [victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu](https://victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu.au/mathematics/mathematics-version-2-0/introduction/structure)

**Language (grammar/vocabulary):**

- Nouns, verbs, adjectives; simple tense; high-frequency spelling patterns. [oaic.gov](https://www.oaic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0020/262631/Exposure-Draft-Childrens-Online-Privacy-Code.pdf)

**Literature:**

- Focus on familiar text types, characters, settings, simple themes. [victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu](https://victoriancurriculum.vcaa.vic.edu.au/mathematics/mathematics-version-2-0/introduction/structure)

**Weekly “new skill” rule (English Years 1–2):**

Each week:

- 1 Reading focus (e.g., “literal comprehension – retrieving explicit information”).
- 1 Writing focus (e.g., “write a simple narrative with beginning–middle–end”).
- 1 Language focus (e.g., “identify nouns and verbs in a sentence”).
- Literature integrated into reading/writing tasks rather than as a separate “skill”.

## 4. Rule-based “weekly new skill selector” you can implement

You can encode this as a set of rules in your backend:

**Inputs:**

- `state` ∈ {VIC, NSW}
- `year` ∈ {1..6}
- `subject` ∈ {Maths, English}
- `week_index` ∈ {1..10} (per term)
- `term_index` ∈ {1..4} (optional, for multi-year planning)

**Outputs:**

- List of “new skills” for this week (with curriculum codes).

### Pseudocode skeleton

```python
def get_weekly_new_skills(state, year, subject, week_index, term_index=1):
    if state == "VIC":
        level = year  # Level 1–6
        if subject == "Maths":
            return get_vic_maths_weekly_skills(level, week_index, term_index)
        else:
            return get_vic_english_weekly_skills(level, week_index, term_index)
    elif state == "NSW":
        stage = map_nsw_stage(year)  # Stage 1/2/3
        if subject == "Maths":
            return get_nsw_maths_weekly_skills(stage, year, week_index, term_index)
        else:
            return get_nsw_english_weekly_skills(stage, year, week_index, term_index)
```

Inside each `get_*_weekly_skills`:

- Use the **week patterns** described above to select:
  - 1 main focus skill (Number/Reading/Writing).
  - 1 secondary skill (Measurement/Space or Language).
  - Optional third skill (Statistics/Probability or Data/Chance) every 2–3 weeks.
- Map each focus to:
  - AC code(s)
  - VIC code(s) or NSW code(s)
  - A `skill_id` in your database.
