## Year 3 Maths – 10-week sequence (VIC & NSW)

**Assumptions:**

- 10-week term, ~3–4 Maths lessons per week.  
- Each week has:
  - 1 **Number/Algebra** focus (main thread).  
  - 1 **Measurement/Space** focus.  
  - **Statistics/Probability** every 2–3 weeks.  
- VIC uses **AC9 codes + VIC phrasing**; NSW maps the same skills to **Stage 2 subareas** (Representing numbers, Additive relations, Multiplicative relations, Partitioned fractions, Geometric measure, 2D/3D spatial structure, Non-spatial measure, Data, Chance). [sproutlessons](https://www.sproutlessons.com/blog/year-3-maths-ac9-codes)

## How to use this in your app

- Store each row as a **weekly focus set** for Year 3 Maths.
- For each focus skill:
  - Create a **canonical skill record** with AC9 codes.
  - Add **state-specific mappings**:
    - VIC: Level 3 phrasing + any local codes.
    - NSW: Stage 2 subarea + placeholder codes (to be finalised against the NSW syllabus). [qcaa.qld.edu](https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/downloads/aciqv9/mathematics/curriculum/ac9_maths_yr3_as_cd_alignment.pdf)
- In your **weekly new skill selector**, for Year 3, Week N:
  - Mark the listed Number, Measurement/Space, and (where applicable) Statistics/Probability skills as “new” for that week.
  - Your spaced-repetition engine then schedules reviews in subsequent weeks according to the logic we defined earlier.
