Below is a **sample 10-week sequence table for Year 3 English**, aligned to both **VIC (Level 3)** and **NSW (Stage 2)**, with example skill descriptions and placeholder curriculum codes.

This mirrors the Maths table structure and is designed so you can mark **1 Reading**, **1 Writing**, and **1 Language** focus as “new” each week, with Literature integrated throughout. [curriculum.nsw.edu](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/english/english-k-10-2022/overview)

***

## Year 3 English – 10-week sequence (VIC & NSW)

**Assumptions:**

- 10-week term, ~3–4 English lessons per week.  
- Each week has:
  - 1 **Reading comprehension** focus (Literacy strand).  
  - 1 **Writing** focus (text type + structural/language features).  
  - 1 **Language** focus (grammar, vocabulary, spelling, punctuation).  
- **Literature** is woven into reading/writing tasks (stories, poems, literary texts) rather than treated as a separate weekly skill. [curriculum.nsw.edu](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/english/english-k-10-2022/overview)
- VIC uses **AC9 codes (Level 3)**; NSW maps to **Stage 2 outcomes** (EN2-RECOM-01, EN2-8B, etc.) and focus areas (reading fluency, reading comprehension, creating written texts, spelling, understanding literature). [curriculum.nsw.edu](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/english/english-k-10-2022/overview)
