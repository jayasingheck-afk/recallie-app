For your specific situation—a solo founder building an evidence-based, curriculum-aligned learning app for Australian primary students—my strong recommendation is:

## Build a **web-based application first**, not a native mobile app

### Why web-first is the right choice for you

**1. Faster iteration and lower complexity**

- You can ship updates instantly without app store review delays. [solodevstack](https://solodevstack.com/blog/best-tech-stack-course-platform-solo-developer)
- One codebase serves all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile) instead of maintaining separate iOS/Android apps. [solodevstack](https://solodevstack.com/blog/best-tech-stack-course-platform-solo-developer)
- As a solo founder, this dramatically reduces your maintenance burden. [alphonsolabs](https://www.alphonsolabs.com/best-web-app-tech-stacks-2026/)

**2. Better for schools and classroom use**

- Schools often block app downloads or have strict device policies; web apps work on any browser. [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)
- Teachers can easily share URLs, embed in LMS (Google Classroom, Seesaw), and manage access via school accounts. [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)
- Easier to offer school licenses and bulk provisioning. [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)

**3. Children actually learn well on web apps**

- Research shows high-quality learning apps (web or mobile) used for just 5–15 minutes daily can improve maths and literacy outcomes. [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11868692/)
- The key is **quality design and evidence-based pedagogy**, not the platform itself. [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8916741/)
- Web apps can be fully responsive and touch-optimized for tablets (which many schools use). [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)

**4. Cost and compliance advantages**

- Hosting in Australian regions (AWS/GCP/Azure Sydney) is straightforward for web apps. [techtweekinfotech](https://techtweekinfotech.com/privacy-act-app-compliance-cloud-management-australia/)
- Easier to implement child privacy compliance (data minimisation, parental dashboards, audit logs). [techtweekinfotech](https://techtweekinfotech.com/privacy-act-app-compliance-cloud-management-australia/)
- Lower infrastructure costs initially; you can start at $0–$50/month and scale. [startupbricks](https://www.startupbricks.in/blog/solo-founder-tech-stack-2025/)

## Recommended tech stack for your EdTech web app (2026)

Given your preferences (React/Next.js + Node/Python, cloud hosting, solo founder), here's the optimal stack:

### **Frontend: Next.js 15 (React) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS**

- **Why:** Best-in-class for SaaS/EdTech in 2026; excellent SEO, fast performance, huge ecosystem. [solodevstack](https://solodevstack.com/blog/best-tech-stack-course-platform-solo-developer)
- **UI components:** Use `shadcn/ui` or similar—copy-paste components you own, no vendor lock-in. [wfnext](https://wfnext.com/blog/best-tech-stack-saas-mvp-2026/)
- **Responsive by default:** Works perfectly on tablets (critical for Years 1–6). [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)

### **Backend: Next.js API routes (Node.js) initially, then NestJS if needed**

- **Why:** One language (TypeScript/JavaScript) across full stack; fastest iteration for solo founders. [solodevstack](https://solodevstack.com/blog/best-tech-stack-course-platform-solo-developer)
- **Start simple:** Next.js API routes are enough for MVP. [solodevstack](https://solodevstack.com/blog/best-tech-stack-course-platform-solo-developer)
- **Scale later:** Migrate complex logic to NestJS (Node.js framework) as you grow. [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)

**Alternative:** If you plan to do heavy AI processing (e.g., real-time Claude API orchestration, custom ML models), consider **Python FastAPI** for the backend while keeping Next.js frontend. [wfnext](https://wfnext.com/blog/best-tech-stack-saas-mvp-2026/)

### **Database: PostgreSQL (managed)**

- **Options:** Supabase, Neon, or AWS RDS/Aurora. [solodevstack](https://solodevstack.com/blog/best-tech-stack-course-platform-solo-developer)
- **Why:** Relational data (users, classes, skills, attempts) fits PostgreSQL perfectly; strong typing with Prisma/Drizzle ORM. [solodevstack](https://solodevstack.com/blog/best-tech-stack-course-platform-solo-developer)
- **Compliance:** Choose a provider with Australian regions (e.g., AWS Sydney `ap-southeast-2`). [techtweekinfotech](https://techtweekinfotech.com/privacy-act-app-compliance-cloud-management-australia/)

### **Authentication: Clerk or Auth0**

- **Why:** Managed auth saves you weeks of work; handles parent/teacher/student roles, SSO, parental consent flows. [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)
- **Child safety:** Built-in support for age-gating, parental consent, and session management. [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)

### **Payments: Stripe**

- **Why:** Industry standard; supports subscriptions (monthly/annual), family plans, school invoices. [solodevstack](https://solodevstack.com/blog/best-tech-stack-course-platform-solo-developer)
- **Australia:** Full support for AUD, local payment methods. [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)

### **Hosting: Vercel (frontend) + AWS/GCP Sydney (backend/database)**

- **Frontend:** Vercel is optimal for Next.js—zero config, edge functions, automatic scaling. [solodevstack](https://solodevstack.com/blog/best-tech-stack-course-platform-solo-developer)
- **Backend/Database:** Host in **AWS `ap-southeast-2` (Sydney)** or **GCP Australia** to comply with Australian Privacy Principles and data residency requirements. [techtweekinfotech](https://techtweekinfotech.com/privacy-act-app-compliance-cloud-management-australia/)
- **Why this split:** Vercel for speed/SEO; Australian cloud for compliance and data control. [techtweekinfotech](https://techtweekinfotech.com/privacy-act-app-compliance-cloud-management-australia/)

### **AI integration: Claude API (Anthropic) + your own orchestration layer**

- **Your workflow:** Use Claude to generate exercise banks (as we designed), store in your database, then serve via your API. [wearebrain](https://wearebrain.com/blog/best-tech-stack-edtech-2026/)
- **Why not real-time AI:** For child safety and compliance, pre-generate and human-review all content; don't let children interact directly with AI. [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8916741/)

### **Analytics: PostHog (self-hosted or cloud)**

- **Why:** Open-source, privacy-focused; tracks user behavior without violating child privacy. [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)
- **Avoid:** Google Analytics for children's data (compliance risk). [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)

## Mobile app? Do this instead

**Don't build native iOS/Android apps at launch.** Instead:

1. **Make your web app a Progressive Web App (PWA):**
   - Works offline (caches recent sessions).
   - Can be "installed" to home screen (looks like an app).
   - No app store approval needed. [solodevstack](https://solodevstack.com/blog/best-tech-stack-course-platform-solo-developer)

2. **Optimize for tablets:**
   - Years 1–6 students often use school tablets (iPad, Chromebook).
   - Ensure touch-friendly UI, large buttons, minimal typing. [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)

3. **Add native apps later (if needed):**
   - Once you have traction and revenue, use React Native or Flutter to build mobile apps from your existing logic.
   - By then, you'll have validated demand and can afford the complexity. [alphonsolabs](https://www.alphonsolabs.com/best-web-app-tech-stacks-2026/)

## Compliance and child safety (critical for Australia)

Your web app must comply with:

- **Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)** and **Children's Online Privacy Code** (OAIC). [techtweekinfotech](https://techtweekinfotech.com/privacy-act-app-compliance-cloud-management-australia/)
- **Data residency:** Host all data in **`ap-southeast-2` (Sydney/Melbourne)** regions. [techtweekinfotech](https://techtweekinfotech.com/privacy-act-app-compliance-cloud-management-australia/)
- **Data minimisation:** Collect only what's necessary; avoid behavioural advertising. [techtweekinfotech](https://techtweekinfotech.com/privacy-act-app-compliance-cloud-management-australia/)
- **Parental consent:** Implement age-gating and parental consent flows for under-13s. [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)
- **Security:** Encryption at rest and in transit; audit logs; regular security reviews. [techtweekinfotech](https://techtweekinfotech.com/privacy-act-app-compliance-cloud-management-australia/)

**Practical steps:**

- Use managed services (Clerk, Supabase, Vercel) that handle much of the security burden. [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)
- Conduct a **Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA)** before launch. [techtweekinfotech](https://techtweekinfotech.com/privacy-act-app-compliance-cloud-management-australia/)
- Document your data flows and CSP (cloud service provider) contracts. [techtweekinfotech](https://techtweekinfotech.com/privacy-act-app-compliance-cloud-management-australia/)

## My opinion: This is the right approach for you

**Web-first is the smartest choice** because:

- **You're solo:** Web apps are faster to build, iterate, and maintain. [solodevstack](https://solodevstack.com/blog/best-tech-stack-course-platform-solo-developer)
- **Your users are schools and families:** Web apps work everywhere without app store friction. [startupik](https://startupik.com/startup-stack-for-edtech-startups/)
- **Your differentiator is pedagogy, not platform:** Spaced repetition, interleaving, curriculum alignment—these work equally well on web. [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11868692/)
- **Compliance is easier:** Australian data residency, child privacy, audit logs—all simpler on web. [techtweekinfotech](https://techtweekinfotech.com/privacy-act-app-compliance-cloud-management-australia/)

**Only consider native mobile apps later** if:

- You have strong demand from parents who want offline-only access.
- You have revenue to fund a separate mobile team or agency.
- You've validated that web adoption is maxed out.
